Always in reach

One click from anywhere

NextUp sits in the menu bar showing today's date — or the time, or just an icon, whichever you prefer. Click it and your day appears.

Turn on the optional second menu bar item and your next event stays visible all day, counting down. Click it to join the call without opening anything else.

Calendar + Reminders

Your whole day, in one list

NextUp merges Apple Calendar and Apple Reminders into a single agenda, so you stop switching apps to see what you actually have to do.

Check reminders off right from the list. Overdue items rise to the top of Today, undated ones sink to the bottom, and a “now” line marks where you are in the day. The meeting you're in gets a coloured ring, a Join button, and how long is left.

Quick entry

Type it the way you'd say it

Type “Lunch with Sam tomorrow 1pm at Cafe Rio” and NextUp fills in the date, the time, and the location — then saves the event as just “Lunch with Sam”. The words it understood are stripped from the title; anything you correct by hand stays put.

Meetings

Never miss a meeting

A banner slides in before your meeting — five minutes ahead by default, or whenever you like. Join in one click, snooze it for 5, 15 or 30 minutes, or jump to the event in Calendar. For the meetings you really can't miss, switch on the full-screen alert.

NextUp finds the call link in the event's URL, location or notes, and knows these services out of the box. Add your own with a name and a domain.

The month view

A month at a glance

Dots under each date show what's booked — one plain dot, or up to three in your calendars' own colours. Pick any day to see its agenda.

Add week numbers, highlight the current week, dim the weekend, or put a permanent stripe behind the weekdays that matter to you. Swipe two fingers to change months if you like.

Search

Find that meeting

Press ⌘F and type. NextUp searches titles, locations, notes and calendar names, and groups what it finds under Today, Tomorrow, Next Week, by month, and Past.

Repeating events collapse to the last one and the next one, so a weekly standup doesn't bury everything else. Arrow keys move, Return opens, Esc closes.

Time zones

Know what time it is there

Track as many time zones as you need in a strip at the bottom of the window, with the local time, the UTC offset, and whether it's already tomorrow.

Choose from around 600 zones and give each one whatever name you actually call it. Toggle the whole strip with ⌘Z.

Make it yours

Made to match your Mac

Six themes, each with a full light and dark palette, plus Light, Dark or System appearance. Set the font size, the row density, and how tall the window is — or just drag its bottom edge.

Choose which calendars and reminder lists appear, which get alerts, and what the menu bar shows.

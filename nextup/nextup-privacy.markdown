# NextUp Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 18, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how NextUp ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use it. NextUp is a macOS menu bar calendar application.

**The short version: NextUp does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. Everything the App does happens locally on your Mac.**

## 1. Information We Access

NextUp requests access to the following macOS system data, solely to provide its core features:

| Data | Why we access it | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Calendars** | To display your upcoming events in the menu bar and agenda | Stays on your device |
| **Reminders** | To display your reminders alongside calendar events | Stays on your device |
| **Contacts** | To suggest addresses when you type a location while creating or editing an event | Stays on your device |

All of this data is read through Apple's EventKit and Contacts frameworks, directly on your Mac. NextUp has no server, no backend, and no network connection through which this data could be sent anywhere. We — the developer — never see it.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

- Your name, email address, or any account information (NextUp has no account or sign-in system)
- Analytics or usage statistics
- Crash reports or diagnostic data
- Advertising identifiers
- Location data (GPS/precise location) — only the text you type into an address field, used solely for local autocomplete suggestions

NextUp contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs).

## 3. Purchases

If you purchase full access to NextUp, that transaction is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. We receive no payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.) — Apple handles this in accordance with its own privacy practices. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for details on how Apple processes App Store purchases.

## 4. Third-Party Links

When you join a video call from an event (e.g., a Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams link found in your calendar event), NextUp opens that link in your browser or the relevant app. Your interaction with that third-party service is governed by its own privacy policy, not this one.

## 5. Data Storage

Any preferences you set in NextUp (such as your chosen theme, which calendars are shown, or window size) are stored locally on your Mac using standard macOS mechanisms (`UserDefaults`). This data never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the App and its associated data.

## 6. Children's Privacy

NextUp is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age, because it does not collect personal information at all.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Since NextUp collects no data, changes are expected to be rare and largely clarifying.

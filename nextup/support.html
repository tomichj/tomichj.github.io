Support

Questions, bug reports, and feature requests all go to the same place, and they're all welcome.

NextUpMenuBarCalendar@gmail.com

To save a round trip, please include your macOS version, your NextUp version (Settings → About), and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Getting started

NextUp has no window of its own — it lives in the menu bar. Click its icon to open the popover; right-click it for Settings… and Quit NextUp. Settings are also reachable from the gear button in the popover header, or with ⌘,, and are organised into General, Menu Bar, Calendar, Events, Reminders, Time Zones, License, and About.

Permissions

NextUp isn't showing any of my events

NextUp needs Calendar access to read your schedule. macOS asks the first time you launch it; if that prompt was declined, grant it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars and switch NextUp on. If events still don't appear, check Settings → Calendar → Calendars to Show — individual calendars can be hidden there.

Why don't I see my reminders?

Reminders are off by default, deliberately: NextUp doesn't ask for Reminders access unless you want the feature. Turn on Settings → Reminders → Show Reminders in Your Agenda and macOS will ask for permission then. If you see the message “NextUp doesn't have access to your reminders. Grant access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Reminders,” access was previously declined and needs to be granted there.

Why does NextUp want my Contacts?

Only to suggest addresses while you type a location into an event. It's optional — decline it and everything else works, you just won't get contact-based address suggestions. Nothing from your contacts is stored or sent to us; see the privacy policy.

Events and reminders

Where did part of my event title go?

That's quick entry doing its job. When you type something like “Dentist friday 3pm”, NextUp reads the date and time, applies them, and removes those words from the title — so you get an event called “Dentist”. Anything it didn't use stays in the title, and if you override a field by hand, the words for that field are left alone. Check the hint row under the title field to see what it understood before you save.

How do I type a time?

The time fields accept 3, 3p, 3:30 pm, 1530, and 930. Four digits are always read as 24-hour time. ↑ and ↓ step by 15 minutes. If what you type isn't a time, the field quietly reverts when you click away.

Editing a repeating event

NextUp asks whether to apply the change to This Event Only or All Future Events, the same choice Calendar offers.

My video call has no Join button

NextUp looks for a meeting link in the event's URL field, its location, and its notes. It knows Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Amazon Chime, GoToMeeting, BlueJeans, Skype, RingCentral, Zoho Meeting, Adobe Connect, Jitsi Meet, Whereby, and FaceTime. For anything else, add it under Settings → General → Add Custom Service… with a name and a domain. Also check that Show join video call button is on in the Events tab.

The window and the menu bar

The window is too tall, or too short

Drag the thumb at the very bottom of the popover to resize the agenda, or set a fixed Window Height in Settings → General → Layout. You can also collapse the calendar with ⌘C or hide the time zone strip with ⌘Z.

The window closes when I click somewhere else

That's the default. Turn on Keep Calendar On Top in Settings → General to make it stay open while you work elsewhere.

Changing what the menu bar shows

Settings → Menu Bar controls all of it: icon only, date, time, or a combination; which glyph the icon uses; the date format; 24-hour time and seconds; and whether to add a second menu bar item showing your next event.

Purchases

What happens when the trial ends?

The 7-day trial unlocks everything. After it expires, viewing stays free — your agenda, the month calendar, search, and time zones all keep working. Creating events and reminders, editing them, joining calls from NextUp, and meeting alerts require the one-time $4.99 purchase.

I already bought NextUp, but a new Mac says trial

Open Settings → License and choose Restore Purchases, while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. If you see “No previous purchase found,” check which account is signed in to the App Store.

Refunds

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too — use Apple's App Store refund process at reportaproblem.apple.com. We aren't able to issue refunds directly. See the terms.

Removing NextUp

Quit NextUp from its menu bar icon and drag it to the Trash. NextUp stores nothing but your own preferences, locally on your Mac; deleting the app and its data removes them. Your calendars and reminders are untouched — they belong to macOS, not to NextUp.
